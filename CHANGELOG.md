# Changelog

Rules added or changed, newest first. Format: date, skill, rule, source.

## 2026-11-15

- product-design-foundations, product-ui-patterns: platform values re-checked against the live guidelines. The Material navigation bar is the flexible navigation bar at 64 (the original 80 bar is no longer recommended); the shape scale gains 20, 32 and 48; window size classes gain large (1200 to 1599) and extra-large (1600 and up); Material motion is spring tokens (standard scheme by default, expressive as a brand decision); iOS bars are system components on Liquid Glass from iOS 26 (floating tab bar, no custom bar backgrounds, no glass in the content layer); tab bars carry labels. Source: m3.material.io and the Human Interface Guidelines, read 2026-11-15.
- typography-craft, product-design-foundations, agency-grade-review: system faces keep their platform tracking (SF Pro's size-specific tracking, Material's per-style letter spacing) and iOS text styles keep Apple's leading; the tracking and 4px line-height rules apply to brand typefaces; light and thin weights at 28 and above only; body leading per platform; dark mode opens the tracking instead of reducing it; the WCAG text spacing rule worded correctly. Source: HIG Typography and the Material type scale tokens, read 2026-11-15.
- design-decision-log, agency-grade-review, prompts/improve-the-skills.md: platform values are re-checked against the live guideline pages when the guidelines change; the review gains a platform currency check. Source: the check above.
- typography-craft: new skill. Typeface selection and pairing, the major second scale on the 4px rhythm, headline and hero figure composition, body and UI text, numerals, micro-typography, rendering and loading, localisation, type as the brand carrier, a 12-point typography review. Source: Marc (typography excellence must be covered) and the visual analysis.
- product-brand-identity: principle 8 (intensity, not volume); the brand device as a carrier and part of decision 10; a direction method (matrix, at least three differing decisions, a different carrier pushed per direction); catalogues of type voices, colour stances, imagery art directions, brand devices, copy voices and convention breaks; default count, intensity and device tests. Source: Marc (is the bespoke style captured?) and the visual analysis.
- product-ui-patterns: four web and marketing patterns (photo hero with uppercase nav, feature section with a device in a brand frame, editorial story block, brand chart). Source: visual analysis.
- agency-grade-review: typography review hook, identity tests, default count and intensity tests, two new visual tells. Source: as above.
- prompts/: improve-the-skills.md (evaluation loop) and calibrate-taste.md (taste calibration from Marc's own work). Source: Marc.
