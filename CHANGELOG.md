# Changelog

## 2026-11-15

- Intent wiring. Intent (installed beside these skills, never copied) owns project context, the problem, flows and navigation, wireframes, words, the state inventory, the usability review and the handoff package; these skills own how the product looks, moves and is built. Order, two reviews in one findings table with one severity scale, one decision record per project (Intent's decisions.md when Intent is installed, DESIGN-DECISIONS.md otherwise), wireframes as structure only. Source: Marc.
- Rules taken from Impeccable and written as these skills' own (credited in NOTICE.md). product-brand-identity: light or dark from the scene of use; the audience world and the rut in the brief; one direction from the audience world, an honest risk per direction, the category standard played straight as a standing alternative; reading surfaces in the brand moments map; the generated looks of the moment; rut and memory tests. product-design-foundations: space above a heading; hue-tinted secondary text on colour; perceptual colour ramps; sample content against invented claims; the parts the browser draws; content visible without script; hover that never moves its own target; loops only for live information. typography-craft: name the face and its reason, the spent faces, steps between roles of 1.25 or 200 heavier, optical trim for labels. agency-grade-review: judge before measuring, capture check, tells test, a fresh reviewer for Full and Pre-ship reviews. kraftwrk-design-style: steering words with a sovereign scope; manufactured contrast in copy. Source: Impeccable 4.9.1 (Paul Bakaus, Apache 2.0), proposed to Marc.
- agency-grade-review: the measured gate as a script (skills/agency-grade-review/scripts: audit.py, measure.js, two fixtures and a self-test). Contrast in every theme, state and language, sampled over imagery; counts; grid; targets; text scaling; a motion inventory; generic tells. Severity gains the audit's Majors. Source: the pass, and Impeccable's published detector rules.
- typography-craft, product-design-foundations, product-ui-patterns: the measure is counted in characters, not in ch. Measured on eight faces at 16: 45ch holds 56 to 67 characters, 65ch holds 80 to 96, a 720 column about 100. Max-width is now 36 to 50ch or 20 to 32rem; the 720 reading column is gone. Source: the audit.
- product-design-foundations: the motion property rule said two things; movement animates transform and opacity, colour may cross-fade as a state effect, layout properties never. DESIGN.md (the open format) as the system file in code projects, written after the first build. Source: the audit, Material 3 effects springs, Google Labs' DESIGN.md format.
- Withdrawn: the story-block measure rule from the first round. The finding behind it (about 40 characters per line on Halewyn) was a miscount; the render has 47 to 54.
- Repo: NOTICE.md (sources and credits), templates/DESIGN.md, .claude-plugin/ (the repo installs as a plugin).
- Improvement pass (prompts/improve-the-skills.md): three test products designed with the skills only, reviewed in Full mode with measured tests, scores in evals/README.md. Rules below come from findings the skills allowed. Source: the pass, plus Apple HIG Motion, Material 3 motion, Emil Kowalski (7 practical animation tips; Good vs great animations), Rauno Freiberg (Invisible details of interaction design) and shipped flows on Mobbin (Uber, Google Pay, KOHO, Headspace).
- product-design-foundations: a Flows section (design the flow, named in-progress states, local failure, success by stakes); inverse and on-image colour roles; readable text never lighter than secondary; locked settings keep their explanation readable; radius logic versus system containers; states change the chrome; web targets 44 high; buttons name the selection and the limiting fact sits above the action; legal microcopy per market; type in rem and text scaling shown; system components in mockups; motion: entrances from 0.9, exits retrace entrances, no animation on functional data, press feedback 0.97, gesture triggers, spring speed by element size, a motion table in every prototype; hero figure may go to 45 or 57 when it is the pushed carrier.
- typography-craft: tracking bands closed (24 to 35, 36 and up), rules for all caps at display sizes and for mono figures; mono only in columns and stand-alone figures; settings labels wrap; steps counted per surface; measure counted in the render; a search for straight quotes before showing; bold reset.
- product-ui-patterns: dashboard home has one filled entry point; a settings row variant; create action placement per platform; the panel pattern follows the radius logic and never matches the screen's canvas; the hero figure is the user's own fact, never a claim; an eyebrow carries a fact the heading does not.
- product-brand-identity: every direction shows one loud brand moment; the matrix has twelve rows; stand-in imagery with a written brief; brand canvas in dark mode; signature motion from static layers, at brand moments only; a default with a reason counts as a decision.
- agency-grade-review: counts from the render, per surface; contrast in both themes and every state, sampled over imagery; state and flow tests; a severity table; thumb test yields to platform convention; web at 390 and 1440; prototype switches.
- evals/: the three briefs and the scores per pass.
- product-design-foundations, product-ui-patterns: platform values re-checked against the live guidelines. The Material navigation bar is the flexible navigation bar at 64 (the original 80 bar is no longer recommended); the shape scale gains 20, 32 and 48; window size classes gain large (1200 to 1599) and extra-large (1600 and up); Material motion is spring tokens (standard scheme by default, expressive as a brand decision); iOS bars are system components on Liquid Glass from iOS 26 (floating tab bar, no custom bar backgrounds, no glass in the content layer); tab bars carry labels. Source: m3.material.io and the Human Interface Guidelines, read 2026-11-15.
- typography-craft, product-design-foundations, agency-grade-review: system faces keep their platform tracking (SF Pro's size-specific tracking, Material's per-style letter spacing) and iOS text styles keep Apple's leading; the tracking and 4px line-height rules apply to brand typefaces; light and thin weights at 28 and above only; body leading per platform; dark mode opens the tracking instead of reducing it; the WCAG text spacing rule worded correctly. Source: HIG Typography and the Material type scale tokens, read 2026-11-15.
