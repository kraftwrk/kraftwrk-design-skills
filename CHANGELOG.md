# Changelog

Rules added or changed, newest first. Format: date, skill, rule, source.

## 2026-11-15

- typography-craft: new skill. Typeface selection and pairing, the major second scale on the 4px rhythm, headline and hero figure composition, body and UI text, numerals, micro-typography, rendering and loading, localisation, type as the brand carrier, a 12-point typography review. Source: Marc (typography excellence must be covered) and the visual analysis.
- product-brand-identity: principle 8 (intensity, not volume); the brand device as a carrier and part of decision 10; a direction method (matrix, at least three differing decisions, a different carrier pushed per direction); catalogues of type voices, colour stances, imagery art directions, brand devices, copy voices and convention breaks; default count, intensity and device tests. Source: Marc (is the bespoke style captured?) and the visual analysis.
- product-ui-patterns: four web and marketing patterns (photo hero with uppercase nav, feature section with a device in a brand frame, editorial story block, brand chart). Source: visual analysis.
- agency-grade-review: typography review hook, identity tests, default count and intensity tests, two new visual tells. Source: as above.
- prompts/: improve-the-skills.md (evaluation loop) and calibrate-taste.md (taste calibration from Marc's own work). Source: Marc.

- design-decision-log: new skill. The learning loop: project decisions to DESIGN-DECISIONS.md, general decisions proposed as rules in the skills. Source: Marc.
- product-brand-identity: the ten decisions that make a product identity; radius logic on the Material shape scale. Source: visual analysis.
- agency-grade-review: visual tells table, pattern check, canvas-and-accent and hero figure tests, presentation conventions. Source: visual analysis.
- kraftwrk-design-style: canvas chosen on purpose, one hero figure per screen, presentation conventions, corrections routed to design-decision-log. Source: Marc.
