# Sources and credits

These skills are Marc Luijkx's own rules. Where a rule or a check was learned from someone else's published work, it is written again in these skills' own words and credited here. No text or code is copied.

## Impeccable

Several rules in product-brand-identity, product-design-foundations, typography-craft, agency-grade-review and kraftwrk-design-style, and several checks and thresholds in the audit (`skills/agency-grade-review/scripts`), follow ideas published in Impeccable: sourcing directions from the audience's own world, the category standard played straight, sample content against invented claims, hue-tinted secondary text on colour, space above a heading, the parts the browser draws, light or dark from the scene of use, hover that never moves its own target, content visible without script, a review that reads the render before the measurements, steering words with a sovereign scope, and the list of spent typefaces and generic tells.

- Work: Impeccable 4.9.1, https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable
- Author: Paul Bakaus
- Licence: Apache License 2.0
